What are the top 3 stocks in my portfolio and why did I choose each one?

These are the three stocks I chose for my portfolio and the reasons why I believe each one is a strong long-term investment.

NVIDIA (NVDA)

I chose NVIDIA because it is highly successful in creating GPUs, which power computers through gaming, but most importantly power AI. Due to the increase in demand for AI, I believe the stock will keep going up similarly to the rest of the stocks in this AI bubble.

It also has a positive trend with a product that is crucial to AI growth, the Blackwell chip. I believe that NVIDIA's stock will continue to grow, and that it is a lower-risk stock because the company is one of the largest in the industry.

TSMC (TSM)

I chose TSMC because it is also highly successful in manufacturing chips. Being one of the world's largest chip manufacturers, it feels like a good investment because of demand from AI.

TSMC produces chips for many major companies like Nvidia, Apple, and AMD. That makes the stock feel like a safe investment with strong growth over time.

Costco (COST)

I chose Costco because it is one of the biggest companies with a strong customer base. Costco's consistent sales, variety of products, and low prices for bulk goods make it hard to beat when compared to other stores.

Costco is a company that I believe will be around for a long time, which is important when investing, because it feels like a safe stock that you can hold without worrying as much.
